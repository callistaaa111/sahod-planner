Privacy policy

Sahod Planner. Last updated October 31, 2026.

Sahod Planner helps you plan your pay, bills, debts, savings and daily spending. This page explains what happens to the numbers you type in. The short version: they stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the app keeps

The app saves the numbers you enter: your pay and paydays, bills and debts, savings percentage, transport and other daily costs, extra income, the money you have now, and the spending you log. It saves them in your phone's browser storage so they're there the next time you open the app.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We don't run any server that receives it, and we can't see it.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • No bank, GCash, Maya or card details. The app never connects to any of them.
  • No location, contacts, photos or camera.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking tools.

Loading the app

When you open Sahod Planner, your phone downloads the app's files from GitHub Pages, and its number font from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services receive basic technical information such as your IP address and device type to deliver the files. None of your budget numbers are included. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Moving your numbers to another phone

If you use Share to another phone, Show QR code or Copy my backup code, the app puts your numbers inside that link or code. The numbers are in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to a website's server. But the link itself carries your numbers, so:

  • Anyone who gets the link or scans the code can load your numbers.
  • The chat or email app you send it through will have a copy of the link.

Only share it with yourself or people you trust.

Deleting your numbers

Go to Setup → Start over → Clear everything. You can also uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. Because we don't keep a copy, numbers that are deleted can't be recovered unless you saved a backup code.

Children

Sahod Planner is made for working adults managing their own pay. It isn't directed at children.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have rights over your personal information. Since your numbers are kept only on your own phone, you can see, change and delete all of them yourself at any time in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
